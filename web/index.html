⚡ Monad Technical Architecture & Gas Guidelines

1. Gas Charged on Limit, Not Gas Used

Unlike Ethereum, Monad asynchronous execution charges based on gas_limit * gas_price. Unnecessarily inflated gas limits waste real MON. MonadQueue hardcodes explicit, tight limits (e.g. 45,000 gas for free claims).

2. 200M Block Gas Limit & 400ms Blocks

Monad features 200M gas per block (6.6x Ethereum) and sub-second finality (800ms). This allows flash queue drops with thousands of concurrent bookings without choking the mempool.

3. Cold Storage & Precompile Pricing

Cold storage read is 8,100 gas (+6,000 vs ETH), and ecrecover precompile is 6,000 gas (2x ETH). MonadQueue caches memory structs to avoid repeated cold SLOADs during bulk operations.
